Corporate policy notice

Because NUMERIQ SOLUTIONS LTD delivers exclusively bespoke high-performance scientific software engineering and technical consulting, once a project milestone is officially initiated we do not accept arbitrary refunds without cause. Settlement is strictly executed against the agreed contractual deliverables.

Pre-commencement cancellation

Cancellation requests received prior to the official kick-off of the first milestone are considered on a case-by-case basis and may be eligible for partial refund of any pre-paid amount, less reasonable mobilisation costs already incurred.

Service quality remediation

If a delivered milestone demonstrably fails to meet the technical acceptance criteria defined in the MSA, NUMERIQ SOLUTIONS LTD will rectify the deliverable at no additional charge.

商业政策声明

鉴于 NUMERIQ SOLUTIONS LTD 交付的产品属于高度定制化的高性能科学计算软件工程与技术咨询，一旦项目里程碑正式签收并启动，本公司不接受任何无故退款。结算将严格依照合同约定的阶段性交付物执行。

启动前取消

对于在首个里程碑正式启动前提出的取消请求，本公司将逐案评估，并可能在扣除已发生的合理动员成本后，部分退还预付款。

质量补救

若已交付里程碑被证明未能满足《主服务协议》中所定义的技术验收标准，NUMERIQ SOLUTIONS LTD 将免费修正相关交付物。

Unternehmensrichtlinie

Da NUMERIQ SOLUTIONS LTD hochgradig maßgeschneiderte Dienste erbringt, akzeptieren wir nach offiziellem Meilensteinbeginn keine willkürlichen Rückerstattungen.

Avis important

NUMERIQ SOLUTIONS LTD fournit des services hautement personnalisés ; après le lancement officiel d'un jalon, aucun remboursement arbitraire n'est accepté.

Aviso importante

NUMERIQ SOLUTIONS LTD ofrece servicios altamente personalizados; una vez iniciado un hito, no se aceptan reembolsos arbitrarios.

重要なお知らせ

NUMERIQ SOLUTIONS LTDは高度にカスタマイズされたサービスを提供しており、マイルストーン正式開始後は任意の返金には応じかねます。

중요 고지

NUMERIQ SOLUTIONS LTD는 고도로 맞춤화된 서비스를 제공하므로 마일스톤이 공식 개시된 이후에는 임의의 환불을 허용하지 않습니다.
